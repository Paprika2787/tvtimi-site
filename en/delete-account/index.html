Delete your TvTimi account

You can delete your TvTimi account and all your data, from the app or without it.

From the app

  • Open Profile, Delete my account.
  • Type DELETE to confirm.
  • Tap Delete permanently.

Without the app

Write to contact@tvtimi.fr from the email address of your account. To check that the request really comes from you, we write to that address in a new message, and your account is only deleted after your reply. We answer you within one month.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address, your password, the app language when you signed up, and your first name if you added it.
  • Your tracking: the shows in your library and their status, the episodes marked as watched, your ratings per episode and per show.
  • Your agreement to the terms: the version of the terms of use and of the privacy policy that you accepted, with the date.
  • The technical logs linked to your account.

When

As soon as your account is deleted, from the app or by us after your confirmation, your account, tracking and agreement data are erased immediately and permanently. Technical logs, which contain neither your email nor your password, are erased automatically after 90 days.
